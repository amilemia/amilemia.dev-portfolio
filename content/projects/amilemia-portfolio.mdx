# Bilingual Portfolio & Lead Engine (amilemia.dev)

A production Next.js 15 portfolio, service catalog, and client intake platform engineered for speed, bilingual accessibility, and verifiable technical quality.

## Key Engineering Highlights

### 1. Zero-Bailout Static Site Generation (SSG)
Every localized route (`/en`, `/fr`, `/projects`, `/services`, `/resources`) is statically pre-rendered at build time with complete HTML content, structured JSON-LD schema, and localized OpenGraph metadata.

### 2. Multi-Step Project Brief Wizard & Lead Magnet Engine
Prospective clients can scope their project through a 4-step Zod-validated intake form (scope, goals, budget tier, timeline) backed by Upstash Redis rate limiting and Resend email notifications, or download technical PDF guides with real-time Redis download tracking.

### 3. Automated Quality & Accessibility Pipeline
Every push is verified through 105+ Vitest unit/integration tests and Playwright E2E accessibility tests verifying keyboard navigation, ARIA landmarks, and bilingual locale switching.
