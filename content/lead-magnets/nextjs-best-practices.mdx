# Next.js Best Practices Toolkit

Review rendering, data boundaries and the release path before your next production deployment.

## Inside the four-page PDF

1. **Define your release context** - Record the version and critical route.
2. **Review the architecture** - Check rendering, caching and trust boundaries.
3. **Verify the risky paths** - Use two small patterns and release checks.
4. **Make a go / no-go decision** - Record blockers, ownership and rollback.

Includes interactive checkboxes and text fields. You can also print a copy.

Targets Next.js 15 App Router, matching this portfolio. Confirm behavior against your installed version, especially on Next.js 16 and later. The two code patterns are focused examples, not a starter repository or a full configuration bundle.
