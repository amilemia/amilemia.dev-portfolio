# Conversion Optimization Guide

## Transform Visitors Into Qualified Inquiries

This guide shares practical architecture and UX strategies for turning a static website into a structured lead-generation engine. No fluff—just actionable patterns you can implement immediately.

### What's Inside:

- **The Conversion Funnel Framework**: Map your visitor journey from landing to inquiry
- **Visual Hierarchy & Value Proposition**: Structuring hero sections for 5-second clarity
- **Copywriting That Converts**: Writing clear, outcome-driven headlines and CTAs
- **Verifiable Trust Signals**: Showcasing real architecture, live demos, and transparent scope
- **Multi-Step Intake Forms**: Reducing form friction with progressive step validation
- **Mobile Touch Ergonomics**: Thumb-friendly targets and sticky navigation patterns
- **Privacy-First Analytics**: Tracking funnel events cleanly with Plausible or Vercel Analytics

### Key Engineering & UX Principles

- **Sub-2s Initial Load**: Fast server-rendered HTML prevents early visitor drop-off
- **Transparent Tiering**: Clear Starter vs. Plus scope comparisons eliminate decision fatigue
- **Structured Intake**: Multi-step project briefs qualify scope and timeline upfront
