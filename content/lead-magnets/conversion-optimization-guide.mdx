# Conversion Optimization Guide

Find the gaps between your offer, your proof and your contact form - then plan one measurable improvement.

## Inside the four-page PDF

1. **Set one conversion goal** - Choose a page, audience and success metric.
2. **Audit the path to inquiry** - Use seven checks to find avoidable friction.
3. **Rewrite the critical moments** - Apply offer, proof and form examples.
4. **Plan your first experiment** - Record a hypothesis and a decision rule.

Includes interactive checkboxes and text fields. You can also print a copy.

For low-traffic sites, start with the usability prompts before planning an A/B test. Examples are starting points, not promises of a conversion uplift.
